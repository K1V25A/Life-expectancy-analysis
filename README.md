 ## Life Expectancy Analysis

 📌 Project Overview

Life expectancy is one of the most important indicators of a country's health and development. This project analyzes global life expectancy data to identify the factors that influence how long people live.

Using data analysis and visualization techniques, the project uncovers patterns and relationships between life expectancy and various socio-economic and health-related factors.

 🎯 Business Problem

Governments and healthcare organizations need to understand which factors have the greatest impact on life expectancy so they can make better policy decisions and allocate resources effectively.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

- Which countries have the highest and lowest life expectancy?
- How has life expectancy changed over time?
- Which factors contribute most to life expectancy?
- What insights can help improve public health outcomes?

 🎯 Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Conduct Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Visualize trends and patterns.
- Analyze relationships between different variables.
- Identify key factors affecting life expectancy.
- Generate meaningful business insights.

 💡 Key Insights

Some important insights obtained from the analysis include:

- Countries with higher GDP generally have higher life expectancy.
- Better education levels are associated with longer life expectancy.
- Higher adult mortality negatively affects life expectancy.
- Healthcare-related indicators show strong relationships with life expectancy.
- Significant improvements in life expectancy have been observed over the years in many countries.
